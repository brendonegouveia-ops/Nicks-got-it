How to play

  • Survive the day. 1 second = 1 minute. The run ends when Chaos hits 100. Your score is the time on the clock.
  • Tap a request bubble, a station, or a room to add it to Nick’s to-do list (max 4). He walks it on his own. Tap a numbered marker (or a to-do chip) to cancel it.
  • When Nick gets there, the Action Panel slides up at the bottom: tap, hold, rub, swipe or mash. He’s stuck there until it’s done. Everyone else’s timer keeps running.
  • One free hand. The other is coffee. Snacks, juice and sippy cups come from the Kitchen; band-aids from the Garage. Coffee under 25% makes Nick 40% slower: hold at the coffee maker to refill.
  • Expired requests make kids cry. Crying kids make everyone else’s timers run 20% faster.
  • Dad Joke (1): freezes every timer for 3 s. Call Kayla (2): she clears one request, twice per run. Because I Said So (3): ends every screen-time argument.
  • 1:00 PM: nap time. Keep the Shhh meter low: barks, fights and smoke alarms wake her.
  • Desktop: mouse, keys 1–3, Space = tap/hold/mash, arrows = swipe, Z/X = choices, U = “uh-huh”, P = pause.
