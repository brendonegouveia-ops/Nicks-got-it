How to play

  • Make it to 5:00 PM. That’s when Kayla’s home and Nick can go to game night. If Chaos hits 100 first, game night’s off. Each week the same Saturday gets harder.
  • Walk with the arrow pad (left thumb). Press between two arrows to walk diagonally. Desktop: WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Help: walk up to a kid, a spill or a station and its job shows on the action pad (right thumb): tap, hold, rub, swipe or mash. Nick can’t walk mid-gesture. Everyone else’s timer keeps running.
  • On my way: grab what a kid asked for and their timer slows down. Holding a snack, drink or band-aid? A crying kid will take it.
  • One free hand. The other is coffee. Snacks, juice and sippy cups come from the Kitchen; band-aids from the Garage. Coffee under 25% makes Nick 40% slower: hold at the coffee maker to refill.
  • Expired requests make kids cry. Crying kids make everyone else’s timers run 20% faster.
  • Dad Joke (1): freezes every timer for 3 s. Call Kayla (2): she clears one request, twice per run. Because I Said So (3): ends every screen-time argument.
  • 1:00 PM: nap time. Keep the Shhh meter low: barks, fights and smoke alarms wake her.
  • Desktop: WASD to walk, keys 1–3 abilities, Space = tap/hold/mash/pick, arrows = swipe (while a swipe is up), Z/X = choices, U = “uh-huh”, P = pause.
